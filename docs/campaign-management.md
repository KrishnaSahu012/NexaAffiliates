# NexaAffiliates — Campaign Management

## 1. Overview

Campaign management connects offers, advertisers, affiliates and traffic sources into an organized marketing workflow.

The NexaAffiliates simulation demonstrates how campaigns can be created, assigned, monitored, reviewed and optimized.

All campaign names, organizations, affiliates and performance information in this document are fictional.

---

## 2. Campaign Management Objectives

The main objectives are:

- Organize campaign records
- Connect advertisers with campaigns
- Assign suitable affiliates
- Track traffic sources
- Monitor campaign status
- Coordinate campaign launches
- Review performance
- Support compliance
- Document follow-up actions

---

## 3. Campaign Structure

A campaign record can contain:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Campaign ID | Unique campaign identifier |
| Campaign Name | Campaign identification |
| Advertiser | Business associated with the campaign |
| Affiliate | Traffic partner associated with the campaign |
| Vertical | Marketing category |
| Traffic Source | Primary acquisition channel |
| Status | Current campaign state |
