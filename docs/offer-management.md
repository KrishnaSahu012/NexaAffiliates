# NexaAffiliates — Offer Management

## 1. Overview

Offer management is a central part of affiliate marketing operations.

The NexaAffiliates simulation demonstrates how offers can be organized, reviewed, filtered and connected with affiliates and campaigns.

All offers and related information in this document are fictional.

---

## 2. Offer Management Objectives

The main objectives are:

- Maintain an organized offer catalog
- Identify suitable traffic sources
- Track CPA/CPL models
- Monitor offer status
- Match offers with affiliates
- Coordinate advertiser requirements
- Support campaign launches
- Monitor compliance requirements

---

## 3. Offer Structure

A typical offer record can contain:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Offer ID | Unique offer identifier |
| Offer Name | Name used to identify the offer |
| Vertical | Marketing category |
| Model | CPA or CPL |
| Payout | Fictional conversion payout |
| Traffic Source | Approved promotional channel |
| Status | Current offer condition |

## 5. CPA and CPL

### CPA

CPA means Cost Per Action.

A conversion occurs when a defined action is completed.

Examples may include:

- Signup
- Registration
- Trial
- Purchase
- Application

---

### CPL

CPL means Cost Per Lead.

The conversion is generally associated with a qualified lead submission.

Examples may include:

- Quote request
- Contact form
- Lead application
- Information request

The exact conversion requirement depends on the offer.
