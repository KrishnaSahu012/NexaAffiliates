# NexaAffiliates — Compliance Workflow

## 1. Overview

Compliance is an important part of affiliate campaign operations.

The NexaAffiliates simulation demonstrates a structured workflow for reviewing campaign requirements, traffic sources, affiliate activity and potential operational risks.

All compliance records in this project are fictional.

---

## 2. Compliance Objectives

The main objectives are:

- Review campaign requirements
- Check traffic sources
- Identify potential policy issues
- Document review results
- Track open actions
- Coordinate with affiliates
- Coordinate with advertisers
- Maintain review history
- Support responsible campaign management

---

## 3. Compliance Review Structure

A fictional compliance review can contain:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Review ID | Unique review identifier |
| Campaign | Campaign being reviewed |
| Affiliate | Partner associated with activity |
| Vertical | Campaign category |
| Risk | Review risk classification |
| Checks | Number of checks performed |
| Passed | Number of checks passed |
| Status | Current review status |
| Last Review | Most recent review date |

---

## 4. Compliance Status

The NexaAffiliates simulation uses four primary statuses.

### Approved

The fictional review has completed the required checks with no open action represented in the simulation.

### Monitoring

The campaign remains active while specific conditions continue to be monitored.

### Action Required

One or more review items require additional attention.

### Pending

The review has not yet reached a final status.

---

## 5. Risk Classification

The simulation uses three risk categories.

### Low

The fictional review currently shows limited operational concerns.

### Medium

Additional monitoring or verification may be appropriate.

### High

The review contains items requiring closer attention before normal activity continues.

Risk classification is a portfolio demonstration concept and does not represent a real compliance scoring system.
