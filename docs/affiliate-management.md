# NexaAffiliates — Affiliate Management

## 1. Overview

Affiliate management is a core responsibility represented in the NexaAffiliates portfolio simulation.

The objective is to demonstrate how an Affiliate Account Manager can organize affiliate information, monitor activity, coordinate campaigns and maintain ongoing communication.

All affiliates and performance information in this document are fictional.

---

## 2. Affiliate Management Objectives

The main objectives are:

- Maintain organized affiliate profiles
- Understand affiliate traffic sources
- Track campaign participation
- Monitor performance activity
- Review traffic quality
- Communicate campaign updates
- Coordinate offer access
- Support compliance requirements
- Identify follow-up actions

---

## 3. Affiliate Profile

A typical affiliate profile can contain:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Affiliate ID | Unique internal identifier |
| Affiliate Name | Partner or media name |
| Primary Contact | Main communication contact |
| Traffic Source | Primary traffic channel |
| Vertical | Main marketing category |
| Status | Current account status |
| Active Campaigns | Current campaign participation |
| Last Activity | Most recent account activity |

## 5. Affiliate Onboarding Workflow

A structured onboarding process can reduce operational issues.

### Step 1 — Profile Review

Review the affiliate's basic information and proposed traffic sources.

Questions may include:

- What traffic sources are used?
- Which verticals are targeted?
- What type of audience is reached?
- Which geographic markets are targeted?
- What promotional methods are planned?

---

### Step 2 — Traffic Source Review

The traffic source should be documented before campaign activation.

Examples:

- Search
- Content
- Social
- Email

The traffic source should be compatible with the campaign's requirements.

---

### Step 3 — Offer Matching

The account manager can identify offers that match:

- Affiliate traffic source
- Geographic market
- Vertical
- Promotional method
- Campaign requirements

---

### Step 4 — Compliance Review

Campaign and traffic requirements should be reviewed before activation.

Examples include:

- Allowed traffic sources
- Promotional restrictions
- Brand restrictions
- Landing-page requirements
- Lead-quality requirements

---

### Step 5 — Campaign Assignment

Once requirements are satisfied, the affiliate can be associated with the relevant campaign.

The campaign record should identify:

- Affiliate
- Advertiser
- Offer
- Vertical
- Traffic source
- Status

---

### Step 6 — Monitoring

After launch, account activity should be monitored.

Important areas include:

- Click volume
- Conversion activity
- Conversion rate
- Traffic quality
- Compliance status
- Communication history
